Our Vision

Mission & Vision

Organized on July 9, 1997, God's Way Christian Baptist Church has grown from a small gathering in Taylor, Texas into a congregation committed to discipleship, evangelism, and whole-person ministry.

Mission

Why we gather

Our mission is transforming our world with God's Word, one life at a time. Our core objectives are to restore those who are hurting, build strong relationships, serve people broadly, evangelize the lost, and strengthen connections between church, community, and schools.
